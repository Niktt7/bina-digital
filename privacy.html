2. Data we collect

  • Contact data you give us: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, company name, and the content of your message.
  • Data you share during a consulting engagement, to the extent needed for the agreed work.
  • Technical data: this website has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers your language choice in your own browser. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and the font service (Google Fonts) receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • If you connect a social media account to an application we operate, such as our Instagram integration, we access only the data you authorise through Meta's platform.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal (your consent and pre-contract steps).
  • To deliver the consulting services we agreed with you (performance of a contract).
  • To keep accounting and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To provide the function of an application you connected, and nothing else.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Sharing

We share your data only when necessary:

  • with a partner (for example a licensing agent or an accountant) when you ask us to introduce you, and only the data needed for that task;
  • with service providers who host our email and files, under their confidentiality and security obligations;
  • with public authorities when the law requires it.

Some providers, such as Google, store data outside Indonesia. Such transfers are made with the safeguards required by UU PDP.

5. Social media integrations

Data received from Instagram or other Meta products is used only for the function you authorised, is never sold or passed to third parties, and is handled in line with the Meta Platform Terms. You can revoke access at any time in your Instagram settings.

6. Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, and accounting records for the period required by Indonesian law. We protect data with access control, encrypted connections and limited access on a need-to-know basis.

7. Your rights

Under UU PDP you may ask us to inform you about the processing of your data, give you access to it or a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, or withdraw your consent. Send your request to info@bigbsc.com. We respond within the time limits set by UU PDP.

8. Data deletion

To delete your data, write to info@bigbsc.com or message us on WhatsApp at +62 813-5350-4430. If you connected an application, you can also remove it in your Instagram settings. Data that we must keep by law is deleted when that period ends.

9. Children and changes

Our services are intended for adults and businesses; we do not knowingly collect data of persons under 18. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
